Burn unit and window

Leave KBDI blank to use the Florida Forest Service value. Use negative numbers to start before sunrise or end after sunset. The outlook takes each day's extremes inside this window.

Prescription

Every criterion is checked hour by hour. An hour is in prescription when the blended forecast meets all of them. Model agreement is the share of models that put that hour in prescription.

Guidance

NBM supplies surface elements only, so it is left out of the mixing height, transport wind and ventilation rows and from model agreement whenever the prescription uses them.

How the numbers are made

Source. Open-Meteo hourly guidance from GFS, ECMWF IFS 0.25°, ICON, GEM and NBM. Each value is the mean of the models selected; the small gray numbers under outlook values are the spread of the same statistic across the models.

VPD (vapor pressure deficit, kPa) is saturation vapor pressure at the 2 m temperature minus actual vapor pressure from the 2 m dew point, as each model reports it. Higher VPD means drier fuels and air that pulls moisture faster.

20 ft winds come from the 10 m wind using a log profile (roughness 0.05 m). Peak winds scale the 10 m gust factor to 20 ft the same way.

Mixing height (ft above ground) is where a surface parcel, 0.5 K warmer than the 2 m temperature, meets the environmental potential temperature profile from the pressure-level data. Transport wind is the mean speed through the mixed layer, from 10 m up to the mixing height, with direction from the mean vector. Ventilation is mixing height times transport wind (m²/s).

KBDI (Keetch-Byram Drought Index, 0–800) starts from the Florida Forest Service's latest county mean for Brevard, saved daily to data/kbdi.json by the site's "Update KBDI" GitHub Action, or from a value you enter. Each later day applies the Keetch-Byram (1968) equation to the blended forecast: drying from the day's high temperature (mean annual rain 50 in by default), and 100 points off per inch of rain after the first 0.20 in of each rain spell. The small numbers are the range from running each model alone.

Planning forecast columns cover each day's burn window, from sunrise and sunset at the point plus the offsets you set. By default it runs 7 days, starting today until the window closes, then tomorrow.
